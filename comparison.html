The American Coney Island Hot Dog has been a favorite with Detroiters and visitors for over a century!

The reasons for the great popularity are the high quality, specially-seasoned, natural skin casing hot dog from Dearborn Sausage and our own Keros family secret recipe Coney Island Chili Sauce developed decades ago.

Add to that a topping of fine mustard and sweet chopped onions — the final touch to the distinctive flavor.

Served in a warm steamed bun, it’s no wonder the American Coney Island Hot Dog has been such a tantalizing treat that’s still hard to beat after all these years!
