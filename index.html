A Century of Detroit Flavor

From Greece to Lafayette Boulevard

In 1903, Constantine "Gust" Keros immigrated to Detroit from Greece. Fourteen years later, in 1917, he opened a small hot dog counter on West Lafayette Boulevard, serving chili-covered hot dogs made with Dearborn Sausage and his own secret recipe chili sauce.

More than a century later, the Keros family still runs the counter. Through the auto boom, the Great Depression, World Wars, and Detroit's renaissance, American Coney Island has stayed true to one simple purpose: the perfect coney dog.

We are Detroit's original coney island, and we are proud to still be here, still family-owned, still serving the same legendary dog.
